# Sound credits

All typewriter sounds are cut, trimmed and peak-normalized from recordings on
[Freesound](https://freesound.org) released under **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**.
Attribution is not required, but we are grateful to the authors:

| File(s) | Source | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `typewriter/key-*.mp3`, `space-*.mp3`, `backspace-*.mp3` | [Typewriter (#164807)](https://freesound.org/people/exterminat/sounds/164807/) | exterminat | CC0 1.0 |
| `typewriter/bell.mp3` | [Typewriter Ding (#204466)](https://freesound.org/people/NHumphrey/sounds/204466/) | NHumphrey | CC0 1.0 |
| `typewriter/return.mp3` | [Typewriter bell & carriage reset (#345955)](https://freesound.org/people/knufds/sounds/345955/) | knufds | CC0 1.0 |

The pack is regenerated with `python3 -I scripts/build_sounds.py <download-dir>` (needs ffmpeg).
The "mechanical" and "soft" sounds are synthesized at runtime and have no source recordings.
